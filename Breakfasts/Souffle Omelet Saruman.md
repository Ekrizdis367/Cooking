## Ingredients

- 3 Eggs #Egg
- 3 ½ Fl Oz Pouring Cream #Dairy
- Salt and Pepper, to Taste #Seasoning #IgnoreIngredient
- ½ oz Butter #Dairy

## Instructions

1. Break your eggs over a bowl, separating the yolks from the whites.
2. Pour the cream into a bowl that has been chilled in the freezer, and beat the cream until thickened.
3. In another bowl, beat the egg yolks with a whisk. Add the whipped cream and mix gently.
4. In another clean bowl, beat the egg whites until firm peaks form, then fold them into the yolk and cream mixture. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Heat the butter in a frying pan over medium heat. When hot, pour in the mixture and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until the omelet is set.
6. Fold the edges toward the center and cook for another 3 to 4 minutes. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- For Extra Flavor, Top with a Sprinkle of Herbs or Grated Cheese.
- Serve with Toasted Bread or Fresh Fruit for a Balanced Breakfast.
