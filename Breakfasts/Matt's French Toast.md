# Ingredients

- Brioche Bread
- 7 eggs
- 4 tbsp sugar
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- 1/4 tsp extract
- 2 cups milk
- maple syrup
- powdered sugar
# Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 200 degrees if cooking on the stove to keep the finished pieces warm.
2. Toast the bread in toaster to avoid becoming soggy.
3. In a large mixing bowl, whisk eggs, sugar, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, and extract thoroughly.
4. Add milk and whisk to blend.
5. Soak bread in egg bath, cook ~3 minutes on one side or until browned.
6. Transfer cooked slices into the oven to keep warm.
7. Serve with butter, powdered sugar, and syrup.
