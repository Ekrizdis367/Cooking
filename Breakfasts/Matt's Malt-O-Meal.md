## Ingredients

- 6 cup Milk
- 2 cup Malt-O-Meal
- 1 pinch Of Salt
- 1/4 Brown Sugar
- 1/4 stick Of Butter

## Instructions

1. Bring milk and salt to a boil/simmer.
2. Pour in Malt-o-Meal and stir continuously.
3. Wait for it to thicken but still be somewhat soupy. It will solidify quickly and you want it to solidify in the bowl.
4. Transfer to bowls and apply butter/brown sugar while still hot - mix together.
