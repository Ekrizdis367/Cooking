## Ingredients

- 4 large Eggs #Egg
- 3 oz Shredded Cheese #Cheese
- 2 small Flour Tortillas (6 inch) #Bread
- Salt and Pepper to taste #IgnoreIngredient

## Instructions

1. Heat a nonstick skillet over medium heat.
2. Beat eggs in a bowl with salt and pepper.
3. Pour eggs into skillet and scramble until mostly cooked through, about 6–8 minutes.
4. Warm tortillas briefly in a dry skillet or microwave.
5. Divide eggs and cheese between the two tortillas, placing filling in the center.
6. Fold in the sides and roll tightly to form a burrito.
7. Serve immediately.
