## Ingredients

- 6 Bacon Strips, Diced
- 2 tbsp Diced Onion
- 3 Medium Potatoes, Cooked and Cubed
- 6 Large Eggs, Beaten
- Salt and Pepper to Taste #IgnoreIngredient
- 1/2 cup Shredded Cheddar Cheese

## Instructions

1. In a cast-iron or other heavy skillet, cook the bacon until crisp. Set it aside on paper towels to drain.
2. Saute the onion and potatoes over medium heat in the bacon drippings until the potatoes are browned, about five minutes.
3. Push the potato mixture to the side of the pan, and pour the eggs into the center of the pan.
4. Cook and stir gently until the eggs are set and cooked to the desired doneness.
5. Stir to combine eggs and potato mixture.
6. Editor's Tip: Use the tips from our best scrambled eggs recipe to make perfectly fluffy eggs.
7. Season the egg mixture with salt and pepper. Sprinkle it with cheese and bacon, and let stand until the cheese melts.
