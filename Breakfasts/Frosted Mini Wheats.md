# Frosted Mini Wheats

Quick, satisfying breakfast cereal bowl. Nutrition estimated from standard serving sizes.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Frosted Mini Wheats Cereal #Grain
- 1 cup Whole Milk #Dairy
- ½ tsp Honey (optional) #Condiment

## Instructions

1. Pour cereal into a bowl.
2. Add milk until cereal is covered.
3. Drizzle with honey if desired.
4. Serve immediately and enjoy.

---

**Notes:** Nutrition is per bowl (1 cup cereal + 1 cup milk). Adjust milk quantity to preference for crunchiness. Add fresh berries for extra nutrition.
