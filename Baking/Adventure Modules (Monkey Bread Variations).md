## Ingredients

- 1 tsp Ground Cinnamon #Seasoning
- ½ cup Granulated Sugar #Baking
- ¼ cup Cold Unsalted Butter #Dairy
- 1 cup Packed Light Brown Sugar #Baking
- 2 (16.3-Ounce) Cans Refrigerated Biscuits (Like Pillsbury Grands) #Bread

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Lightly coat a Bundt pan with nonstick cooking spray.
2. In a small bowl, combine cinnamon and granulated sugar.
3. Separate biscuits and cut each into quarters. Roll each piece in the cinnamon-sugar mixture until evenly coated.
4. Arrange biscuit quarters evenly in the prepared Bundt pan.
5. In a small saucepan, combine butter and brown sugar and bring to a boil over medium heat.
6. Pour syrup mixture evenly over biscuits.
7. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes, until golden brown. Remove from oven and let cool for 5 minutes before inverting onto a serving plate. Serve warm.

## Notes

- For a “module of the Garlic Variety,” Skip the Cinnamon and Sugar, and Instead Roll Biscuit Pieces in Melted Butter Mixed with Garlic, Parmesan, and Herbs for a Savory Twist.
- Best Served Warm and Shared Among Friends After a Successful Campaign.
