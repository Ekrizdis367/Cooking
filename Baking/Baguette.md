## Ingredients

- [ ] 283g water, lukewarm #Pantry
- [ ] 481g sourdough starter #Baking
- [ ] 540g - 600g All-Purpose Flour #Baking
- [ ] 15g salt #Seasoning
- [ ] 8g sugar #Baking
- [ ] 4-8g yeast #Baking
- [ ] 12g vital wheat gluten #Baking

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, combine the water, starter, and **360g** of the flour, mixing until smooth.
2. Stir in the salt, sugar, yeast, and gluten, then an additional **180g - 240g** of flour. Stir until the dough pulls away from the sides of the bowl, adding only enough additional flour as necessary; a slack (sticky) dough makes a light loaf.
3. Knead the dough for about 8 to 10 minutes by hand, on a lightly floured work surface.
4. Turn the dough into an oiled bowl, cover the bowl, and let the dough rise until doubled in bulk, about 1 1/2 hours.

---
   
5. Gently deflate the dough, and divide it into 3 equal pieces.
6. Shape each piece into a 16" long loaf.
7. Cover the loaves and let them rise for 1 1/2 to 2 hours, or until they're nice and puffy. Towards the end of the rising time, preheat your oven to 450°F.

---

8. Bake the baguettes for about 25 minutes, or until they're a rich golden brown.
9. Remove the sourdough baguettes from the oven, and cool them completely on a rack.
