# Ingredients

- [ ] 4 cups Rolled Oats #Baking
- [ ] 1 1/2 cups Nuts #Pantry
- [ ] 1 tsp Salt #Seasoning
- [ ] 1 tsp Cinnamon #Seasoning
   
- [ ] 1/2 cup Coconut Oil #Pantry
- [ ] 1/2 cup Honey #Pantry
- [ ] 1/2 cup Maple Syrup #Pantry
- [ ] 1 tsp Extract #Baking
- [ ] 1/2 cup Coconut Flakes #Pantry

# Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees.
2. Mix together oats, nuts, salt, and cinnamon.
3. Add coconut oil, syrup, honey, and extract. Mix until every oat is covered.
4. Spread in even layer across non-stick pan.
5. Bake for 12 minutes and then stir - add in Coconut Flakes.
6. Bake an additional 12 minutes.
7. Let stand a minimum of 45 minutes. Store in airtight container at room temp.
