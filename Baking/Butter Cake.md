# Ingredients

Cake

- [ ] 2 sticks Unsalted Butter, Softened #Dairy
- [ ] 1 cup Granulated Sugar #Baking
- [ ] 3 Eggs (Room Temperature) #Dairy
- [ ] 1 tsp Vanilla Extract #Baking
- [ ] 1 tbsp Sour Cream (Optional) #Dairy
- [ ] 1 1/2 cups All-Purpose Flour #Baking
- [ ] 1 tsp Baking Powder #Baking
- [ ] 1/2 cup Heavy Cream #Dairy

Sauce

- [ ] 4 oz Cream Cheese, Softened #Dairy
- [ ] 2 tbsp Unsalted Butter #Dairy
- [ ] 1/2 cup Heavy Cream #Dairy
- [ ] 1/4 cup Powdered Sugar #Baking
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Vanilla Extract #Baking

Ice cream

- [ ] 1 cup Sour Cream #Dairy
- [ ] 1 cup Heavy Cream #Dairy
- [ ] 1/2 cup Whole Milk #Dairy
- [ ] 2/3 cup Granulated Sugar #Baking
- [ ] 1 tsp Vanilla Extract #Baking

Whipped cream

- ½ cup (4 ounces / 115 g) mascarpone
- ½ cup plus 2 tablespoons cold heavy cream or whipping cream
- 1 tablespoon organic granulated sugar, maple syrup, or honey
- ½ teaspoon vanilla paste, extract, or seeds from ½ a vanilla bean
- 1 pinch salt

# Instructions

### Sour Cream Ice Cream

- Whisk together sour cream, heavy cream, milk, sugar, vanilla, salt until sugar dissolves.
- Chill, then churn in ice cream maker according to manufacturer’s directions. 
- Freeze until firm.

### Mascarpone Whipped Cream

https://bojongourmet.com/5-minute-mascarpone-whipped-cream/


- Whip heavy whipping cream, sugar, and vanilla until soft peaks form. 
- Keep chilled until ready.

- In the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with the whip attachment (or in a large bowl with an electric beater or whisk), combine the mascarpone, cream, sugar, vanilla, and salt. 
    
- Whip on high until soft peaks form – i.e., when you lift the whisk out and hold it upside down, a peak of cream flops over itself. This usually takes only **1-2 minutes**, but watch it closely the whole time, stopping the mixer if you see any signs of the cream turning grainy or separating, both signs of over-whipping. 
    
- **TBG Tip:** If you accidentally over-whip the cream and it turns grainy, don't fret! You can rescue it by gently folding in a splash of heavy cream, 2-4 tablespoons, until it smooths back out again. 
    
- Use right away, or cover and refrigerate for up to 2 days.

### Butter Cake

- Preheat oven to about 350°F (175°C). Butter and flour a suitable baking dish. A round cake pan or individual ramekins will work; ramekins will allow you to serve warm more easily.
- Beat softened butter and sugars until light and fluffy. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each. Mix in vanilla and sour cream (if using).
- In another bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt. Gradually alternate adding dry ingredients and milk/cream to the butter mixture, beginning and ending with the dry. Don’t overmix once flour is added — just until incorporated.
- Pour into prepared pan, bake until edges are golden and center is set but still moist (you want it warm and soft, not dry) — maybe 25-35 min for a medium pan; shorter if using ramekins (~15-20 min).

### Cream Cheese Sauce

- In a saucepan over low/medium heat, melt butter, then add cream cheese and gentle stirring until smooth.
- Stir in heavy cream, vanilla, and powdered sugar. Heat until warmed through, whisking to remove lumps. Don’t let boil too vigorously — you want a smooth, pourable sauce.



> [!notes] Adjustments & Notes
> - **Texture**: If the version at Rare Society is a bit more gooey in the center, you might underbake slightly or bake in a way that the center remains quite moist. If it’s more like a standard butter cake, aim for fully set.
> - **Butter amount**: Emphasize butter (both for flavor and richness). Can use high-quality unsalted butter. Some recipes add melted butter on top or brush with butter after baking for shine.
> - **Tang**: The sour cream elements (ice cream) and possibly a little sour cream in batter help cut richness. If you want more balance, include a bit of sour cream in the batter or a small tangy component in the sauce.
> - **Cream cheese sauce sweetness**: Match sweetness so it’s rich but not cloying; cream cheese is slightly tangy, so sugar vs cream balance is important.
