## Ingredients

- 250g **all-Purpose Flour** #Baking
- 100g **granulated Sugar** #Baking
- 1/2 tsp **salt** #Seasoning
- 2 And 1/2 Teaspoons **baking Powder** #Baking
- 1/2 cup ; 8 Tbsp; 113g **unsalted Butter**, Frozen #Dairy
- 1/2 cup (120ml) **heavy Cream** or **buttermilk** (Plus 2 Tbsp for Brushing) #Dairy
- 1 Large **egg** #Dairy
- 1 And 1/2 Teaspoons **pure Vanilla Extract** #Baking
- 1–1.5 Cups **add-Ins** Such as Chocolate Chips, Berries, Nuts, Fruit, Etc #Pantry
- **optional:** Coarse Sugar for Topping #Baking

## Instructions

1. Whisk flour, sugar, salt, and baking powder together in a large bowl.
2. Grate the frozen butter using a box grater.
3. Add it to the flour mixture and combine with a pastry cutter, two forks, or your fingers until the mixture comes together in pea-sized crumbs.
4. See video above for a closer look at the texture.
5. Place in the refrigerator or freezer as you mix the wet ingredients together.
6. Whisk 1/2 cup heavy cream, the egg, and vanilla extract together in a small bowl.
7. Drizzle over the flour mixture, add the add-ins, then mix together until everything appears moistened.
8. **To make triangle scones:** Pour onto the counter and, with floured hands, work dough into a ball as best you can.
9. Dough will be sticky.
10. If it’s too sticky, add a little more flour.
11. If it seems too dry, add 1-2 more Tablespoons heavy cream.
12. Press into an 8-inch disc and, with a sharp knife or bench scraper, cut into 8 wedges.
13. For smaller scones, press dough into two 5-inch discs and cut each into 8 wedges. **To make 10-12 drop scones:** Keep mixing dough in the bowl until it comes together.
14. Drop scones, about 1/4 cup of dough each, 3 inches apart on a lined baking sheet. **To make mini (petite) scones, see recipe note.**
15. Brush scones with remaining heavy cream and for extra crunch, sprinkle with coarse sugar. (You can do this before or after refrigerating in the next step.)
16. Place scones on a plate or lined baking sheet (if your fridge has space!) and refrigerate for at least 15 minutes.
17. Meanwhile, preheat oven to 400°F (204°C).
18. Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper or silicone baking mat(s).
19. If making mini or drop scones, use 2 baking sheets.
20. After refrigerating, arrange scones 2-3 inches apart on the prepared baking sheet(s).
21. Bake for 18-26 minutes or until golden brown around the edges and lightly browned on top.
22. Larger scones take closer to 25 minutes.
23. Remove from the oven and cool for a few minutes.
24. Feel free to top with any of the toppings listed in the recipe Note below.
25. Leftover scones keep well at room temperature for 2 days or in the refrigerator for 5 days.
26. ---
27. > [!notes]
28. > 1. **Freeze Before Baking:** Freeze scone dough wedges on a plate or baking sheet for 1 hour.
29. Once relatively frozen, you can layer them in a freezer-friendly bag or container.
30. Bake from frozen, adding a few minutes to the bake time in the recipe below.
31. Or thaw overnight, then bake as directed.
32. >2. **Freeze After Baking:** Freeze the baked and cooled scones before topping with icing or confectioners’ sugar.
33. I usually freeze in a freezer-friendly bag or container.
34. To thaw, leave out on the counter for a few hours or overnight in the refrigerator.
35. Warm in the microwave for 30 seconds or on a baking sheet in a 300°F (149°C) oven for 10 minutes.
36. >3. **Overnight Instructions:** Prepare scones through step 4. Cover and refrigerate overnight. Continue with the recipe the following day.
37. >4. **Scone Flavors:** See blog post above.
38. If adding fruit, use fresh or frozen.
39. If frozen, do not thaw.
40. Peel fruits such as apples, peaches, or pears before chopping.
41. If desired, add 1 teaspoon of cinnamon with the flour.
42. I usually add cinnamon when making chocolate chip scones.
43. >5. **Over-spreading:** Start with very cold scone dough.
44. Expect some spread, but if the scones are over-spreading as they bake, remove from the oven and press back into its triangle shape (or whatever shape) using a rubber spatula.
45. >6. **Optional Toppings:** [Vanilla icing](https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/vanilla-icing/), [salted caramel](https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/homemade-salted-caramel-recipe/), lemon icing from this [iced lemon pound cake](https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/iced-lemon-pound-cake/), maple icing from these [banana scones](https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/banana-nut-scones-with-maple-glaze/), brown butter icing from these [pistachio cookies](https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/pistachio-cookies/), [lemon curd](https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/how-to-make-lemon-curd/), orange icing from these [hot cross buns](https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/hot-cross-buns/), raspberry icing from these [mini pound cakes](https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/mini-vanilla-pound-cakes/), dusting of confectioners’ sugar.
