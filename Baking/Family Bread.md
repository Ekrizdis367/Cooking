# Ingredients

- [ ] Yeast #Baking
- [ ] 1/4 cup lukewarm water #Pantry
- [ ] 1/3 cup crisco #Pantry 
- [ ] 2 tbsp sugar #Baking
- [ ] 4 tsp salt #Seasoning
- [ ] 2 cups scalded milk #Dairy
- [ ] 1 3/4 cups water #Pantry
- [ ] 11 cups all purpose flour #Baking

# Instructions

1. Sprinkle yeast into lukewarm water. Let stand until thoroughly dissolved (5-15) minutes.
2. Place crisco, sugar, and salt into a very large mixing bowl; add scalded milk & stir until crisco is melted 
3. Add water & cool to lukewarm.
4. Add proofed yeast & mix well.
5. Add flour gradually, kneading.
6. Knead dough on floured surface until smooth. Sprinkle flour as needed to prevent sticking. 
7. Place in greased bowl (you can reuse same bowl), brush with crisco, cover & let rise in a warm place until doubled - 1 1/2 hours.
8. Shape dough into 3 loaves & place into bread pans. Brush again with melted crisco & let rise until doubled again for - 45 minutes.
9. Bake in preheated oven @ 400*F - 15 minutes.
10. Reduce to 350 & bake 45 more minutes.
11. Remove from pans, brush with butter, and let cool.
