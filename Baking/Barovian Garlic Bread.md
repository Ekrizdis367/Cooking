## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp Unsalted Butter #Dairy
- 3 clove Minced Garlic #Produce
- 1 tbsp Honey #Condiment
- 1 tsp Kosher Salt #Seasoning #IgnoreIngredient
- 1 lb Pizza Dough (Store-Bought or Homemade) #Bread
- ¼ cup Finely Grated Parmesan Cheese #Dairy
- 1 tbsp Chopped Fresh Parsley #Herb

## Instructions

1. With a rack in the upper-middle position, preheat the oven to 475°F. Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. In a small saucepan over medium-low heat, melt the butter.
3. Add the garlic, honey, and salt, and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
4. Remove from heat and set aside.
5. Divide the pizza dough into 12 equal portions and roll each piece into a rope about 8 inches long.
6. Tie each rope into a loose knot, tucking the ends underneath.
7. Place the knots on the prepared baking sheet.
8. Brush the knots generously with half of the garlic butter mixture. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes, until golden brown.
9. Remove from the oven and immediately brush the warm knots with the remaining garlic butter. Sprinkle with Parmesan and parsley.
10. Serve warm.

## Notes

- For Extra Flavor, Try Adding a Pinch of Red Pepper Flakes or Smoked Paprika to the Garlic Butter Mixture.
