## Ingredients

- 1 lb Spaghetti Noodles #Pasta
- 2 jar Marinara Sauce #Sauce
- 1 lb Italian Sausage Links #Meat
- 4 clove Garlic #Produce
- 1 loaf Garlic Bread #Bread
- 1/2 cup Parmesan Cheese #Cheese

## Instructions

1. Cook spaghetti according to package directions, drain.
2. Brown ground beef in a large skillet, breaking it up as it cooks.
3. Add minced garlic and cook 1 minute.
4. Stir in marinara sauce and simmer 10 minutes.
5. Serve sauce over spaghetti with parmesan cheese and garlic bread.
