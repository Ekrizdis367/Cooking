# Ingredients

- [ ] 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil #Oil  
- [ ] 1 large yellow onion, chopped #Produce  
- [ ] 2 medium leeks, white and light green parts only, chopped #Produce  
- [ ] 3 cloves garlic, minced #Produce  
- [ ] 2 tablespoons tomato paste #Condiment  
- [ ] 1 teaspoon dried thyme #Herb  
- [ ] ½ teaspoon saffron threads #Seasoning  
- [ ] ¼ teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes #Seasoning  
- [ ] 1 cup dry white wine #Beverage  
- [ ] 1 (14.5-ounce) can diced tomatoes, with juices #Produce  
- [ ] 4 cups seafood stock or clam juice #Broth  
- [ ] 2 bay leaves #Herb  
- [ ] 1 pound firm white fish (such as halibut or cod), cut into 2-inch pieces #Seafood  
- [ ] 1 pound mussels, scrubbed and debearded #Seafood  
- [ ] 1 pound clams, scrubbed #Seafood  
- [ ] ½ pound shrimp, peeled and deveined #Seafood  
- [ ] 2 tablespoons Pernod or other anise-flavored liqueur (optional) #Beverage  
- [ ] ¼ cup chopped fresh parsley #Herb  
- [ ] Crusty bread, for serving #Bread  

# Instructions

1. In a large Dutch oven over medium heat, warm the olive oil until shimmering. Add the onion, leeks, and garlic and cook, stirring often, until softened, about 8 minutes.  
2. Stir in the tomato paste, thyme, saffron, and red pepper flakes, and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.  
3. Add the wine, diced tomatoes with juices, seafood stock, and bay leaves. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer and cook for 20 minutes to allow flavors to meld.  
4. Add the fish and cook for 5 minutes. Add the mussels, clams, and shrimp, cover, and cook until the shellfish open and the shrimp are opaque, about 5 minutes more. Discard any shellfish that do not open.  
5. Stir in the Pernod (if using) and parsley. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper.  
6. Serve immediately in warm bowls with crusty bread for dipping.  

# Notes

- For a richer broth, use homemade seafood stock and add a pat of butter before serving.  
- This dish pairs beautifully with a chilled white wine or light ale.
