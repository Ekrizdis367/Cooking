## Ingredients

- 2 Racks Baby Back Ribs (About 3-3.5 Lbs Each, Find Meaty Ones)
- 4 tbsp Yellow or Dijon Mustard
- 1 cup Of Your Favorite BBQ Sauce (for Brushing, Plus More for Serving)
- ½ Of a Large Yellow Onion (Thinly Sliced)
- 6 tbsp Butter (Cut into Pieces)
- 2 tbsp Brown Sugar (Light or Dark)
- 1 tbsp Smoked Paprika (Use "smoked" Paprika, Not Regular)
- 1 tbsp Chili Powder
- 1 tbsp Garlic Powder
- 2 tsp Ground Cumin
- 2 tsp Onion Powder
- 1 tbsp Kosher Salt #IgnoreIngredient
- 1 tsp Ground Black Pepper #IgnoreIngredient

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 275ºF. We’re going for a low oven temperature here.
2. Make the dry rub for the ribs: In a bowl, mix all the dry rub ingredients until evenly combined. Set aside.
3. Prep the ribs: Remove the outer membrane from the back side of the rack of ribs.
4. Here’s how - on one end of the ribs, run a paring knife through the outer membrane layer until the layer starts to loosen and separate.
5. Then carefully peel off the membrane all the way to the other side (use paper towel if needed for a better grip).
6. Discard the membrane.
7. Season ribs: Brush the ribs on both sides with mustard, I use about 1 tablespoon of mustard per side.
8. The mustard will help the seasoning stick.
9. Apply the dry rub over the ribs generously on all sides, pressing lightly to adhere.
10. Assemble: Stack 3-4 large layers of aluminum foil on top of each other, over a large sheet pan.
11. This prevents leaking and promotes better heat retention.
12. Next, place the onions on top of the foil, followed by a rack of ribs, meaty side side up.
13. Scatter about 3 tablespoons of butter on top of the ribs evenly.
14. Seal the foil packets very tightly.
15. Repeat this process with the second rack of ribs.
16. Bake: Arrange both of the racks of robs on a large sheet pan, or use 2 sheet pans.
17. Make sure it fits, they shouldn't hang over the pan.
18. Bake at 275ºF for 3 hours.
19. When ready, open the packets, they should be tender by now.
20. If not, they need more time.
21. Brush with BBQ sauce and broil: Brush the ribs generously with BBQ sauce, then broil for 3-5 minutes until slightly charred and caramelized on top. Enjoy!
