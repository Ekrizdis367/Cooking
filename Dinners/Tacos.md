## Ingredients

- 3 cup Fresh Orange Juice, or More as Needed
- 2 tbsp Finely Chopped Fresh Cilantro
- 2 tbsp Freshly Squeezed Lime Juice
- 1 tsp Minced Garlic
- 1 tsp Salt #IgnoreIngredient
- 1 tsp Ground Black Pepper #IgnoreIngredient
- 1 tsp Ground Cumin
- 1 pinch Cayenne Pepper
- 3 lb Trimmed Skirt Steaks
- 16 (6 Inch) White Corn Tortillas, or as Needed
- 1 White Onion, Chopped, or to Taste
- 1 Small Bunch Fresh Cilantro, Chopped, or to Taste

## Instructions

1. Combine orange juice, 2 tablespoons cilantro, lime juice, garlic, salt, pepper, cumin, and cayenne in a large freezer bag.
2. Submerge skirt steaks in the marinade.
3. Seal the bag, squeezing out as much air as possible.
4. Refrigerate, 1 to 8 hours.
5. Preheat a grill to medium-high.
6. Grill the steaks until hot and firm in the center or to your preferred doneness, 6 to 8 minutes per side.
7. Transfer steaks to a flat work surface and slice as thinly as possible against the grain.
8. Heat corn tortillas on the grill or in a microwave. Keep tortillas warm in a folded sheet of aluminum foil covered by a thick towel.
9. Add steak to each tortilla and top with white onion and cilantro.
