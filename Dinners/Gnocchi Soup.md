## Ingredients

- 2 chicken breasts
- 1/2 cup onion
- 4-6 garlic cloves
- 4 tbsp butter
- 2 cups carrots, diced
- 1 cup celery, diced
- 2 tbsp flour
- 4 cups chicken broth
- Parmesan cheese 
- 2 cups heavy cream

## Instructions

1. — season chicken with garlic powder, paprika, salt, pepper, italian seasonings
2. — cook on each side 6-8 mins or until internal temp reads 165
3. — set aside to cool and then chop into bite sized pieces
4. — in the same pot, melt butter and cook onion and garlic until garlic becomes fragrant
5. — add in celery and carrot and cook for another minute then add in flour and stir for about a minute
6. — add in chicken broth and heavy cream and mix well
7. —this is a great time to taste for seasonings, i added in about 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp italian seasoning and 1/2 tbsp chicken boullion and lots of black pepper
8. — add in spinach and gnocchi and cook for 2-3 mins then add in the chicken and parmesan cheese
