## Ingredients

- CHICKEN:
- 1-1/2 Cups All-Purpose Flour
- 1/2 cup Cornstarch
- 1/2 tbsp Salt #IgnoreIngredient
- 1/2 tsp Pepper #IgnoreIngredient
- 1 Large Egg, Room Temperature
- 1-1/4 Cups Sparkling Water, Chilled
- 1 tbsp Sesame Oil
- 1-1/2 Pounds Boneless Skinless Chicken Thighs, Cut into 1-In. Pieces
- Oil for Deep-Fat Frying
- SAUCE:
- 1 tbsp Canola Oil #IgnoreIngredient
- 1/2 tsp Crushed Red Pepper Flakes
- 1 tbsp Minced Garlic
- 1-1/2 Teaspoons Minced Fresh Gingerroot
- 2 tbsp Sugar
- 2 tbsp Light Brown Sugar
- 2 tbsp Rice Vinegar
- 2 tbsp Orange Juice
- 1 tbsp Reduced-Sodium Soy Sauce
- 1 tsp Grated Orange Zest
- 1 tbsp Water #IgnoreIngredient
- 1 tbsp Cornstarch
- Hot Cooked Rice, Sliced Green Onions and Toasted Sesame Seeds

## Instructions

1. Combine the flour, cornstarch, salt and pepper in a large bowl.
2. Then, whisk in the egg, sparkling water and 1 tablespoon sesame oil until smooth.
3. Editor's Tip: The mixture should resemble a thick pancake batter.
4. If it's too thick, add additional sparkling water.
5. If it is too thin, add extra flour, 1 tablespoon at a time, until the desired thickness is achieved.
6. Add the chicken pieces to the batter and fold them into the mixture until each piece is evenly coated.
7. Cover the bowl, transfer to the refrigerator and let the chicken soak in the batter for at least 30 minutes.
8. Editor's Tip: Resting the chicken in the batter is important because it allows the flour to properly hydrate, which in turn helps the batter adhere better to the chicken.
9. This way, each piece of chicken is evenly coated and perfectly crispy once cooked.
10. Next, heat 1/2 to 1 inch oil to 350°F in a large wok.
11. Working in batches, add a few pieces of the battered chicken to the preheated oil.
12. Cook, turning occasionally, until the chicken is deeply golden on all sides and cooked through.
13. Each batch should take about five to six minutes.
14. Transfer the fried chicken pieces to a paper towel-lined sheet pan to drain excess oil.
15. Repeat with the remaining chicken pieces.
16. Editor's Tip: If you don't own a wok, any deep vessel will work—it just needs to be able to hold the oil for frying.
17. A Dutch oven, stockpot or even a deep skillet would suffice.
18. As your last batch of chicken cooks, start the sauce.
19. Warm 1 tablespoon canola oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
20. Add the red pepper flakes, garlic and ginger, and cook until fragrant, 30 to 60 seconds.
21. Editor's Tip: Don't walk away during this step.
22. If you accidentally burn your garlic (which is not at all the same as roasted garlic), toss everything and start fresh.
23. Otherwise, your sauce will have a bitter flavor.
24. Add the sugar, brown sugar, rice vinegar, orange juice, soy sauce and orange zest. Stir until the sugars dissolve and the mixture begins to bubble.
25. Finish the sauce by whisking together 1 tablespoon cornstarch with 1 tablespoon water in a small bowl.
26. Add it to the wok and whisk until smooth.
27. Cook the sauce until it thickens, one to two minutes.
28. Editor's Tip: Ideally, you're looking for a consistency like honey.
29. If you accidentally thicken the sauce too much, you can thin it back out with a bit of orange juice, water or chicken stock.
30. Add the fried chicken to the sauce and gently toss until every inch of the chicken pieces is coated in the sweet and spicy orange sauce.
31. Serve immediately with hot, cooked fluffy rice, and garnish with green onions and sesame seeds if desired.
