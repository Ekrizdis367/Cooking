## Ingredients

- 1 Onion #Produce
- 24 oz Potatoes #Produce
- 2 lbs Ground Beef #Meat
- 4 slice Cheese #Dairy
- 4 Potato Buns #Bread
- 2 tbsp Old Bay Seasoning #Seasoning
- 4 tsp Dijon Mustard #Condiment
- 4 tbsp Ketchup #Condiment
- 4 tbsp Mayonnaise #Condiment
- 1 tsp Sugar #Baking

## Instructions

1. Adjust rack to top position and preheat oven to 425°F.
2. Wash and dry produce. Cut potatoes into wedges and slice onion.
3. Toss potatoes in a bowl with olive oil and old bay seasoning. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Roast potatoes on baking sheet on top rack for 20-25 minutes.
5. Heat a large drizzle of oil in a medium, preferably nonstick pan, over medium-high heat.
6. Add onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until browned and softened, 8-10 minutes.
7. Add sugar and a splash of water to onions. Cook until caramelized and jammy, 2-3 minutes. Season with salt and pepper, then transfer to plate.
8. In a small bowl, combine mayonnaise, ketchup, mustard, and old bay seasoning to make burger sauce.
9. Form ground beef into 4 equal size balls. Season with salt and pepper.
10. Heat a drizzle of oil in the same pan and add beef balls.
11. Firmly flatten each ball with a spatula to create very thin patties.
12. Cook patties to desired doneness, about 2-3 minutes per side.
13. In the last 1-2 minutes of cooking, top each patty with a slice of cheese.
14. Halve and toast buns.
15. Assemble burgers with sauce, patties, and caramelized onions. Serve with roasted potato wedges.
