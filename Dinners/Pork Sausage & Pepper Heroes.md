## Ingredients

- 24 oz Potatoes #Produce
- 2 Long Green Peppers #Produce
- 1 Onion #Produce
- 2 tsp Garlic Powder #Seasoning
- 36 oz Italian Pork Sausage Mix #Meat
- 2 Tomato Paste #Canned
- 1 tbsp Italian Seasoning #Seasoning
- 4 Demi-Baguettes #Bread
- 1 cup Mozzarella Cheese #Cheese

## Instructions

1. Adjust racks to top and middle positions and preheat oven to 425 degrees. Wash and dry produce.
2. Cut potatoes into 1⁄2-inch-thick wedges.
3. Halve, core, and thinly slice green pepper into strips.
4. Halve, peel, and thinly slice half the onion (whole onion for 4 servings).
5. Place 2 TBSP butter (4 TBSP for 4) in a small microwave-safe bowl; microwave until just softened, 10-15 seconds.
6. Stir in 1⁄4 tsp garlic powder (1⁄2 tsp for 4) and a pinch of salt.
7. (You’ll use the remaining garlic powder in the next step.)
8. Toss potatoes on a baking sheet with a drizzle of olive oil, remaining garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
9. Roast on top rack until browned and crispy, 20-25 minutes.
10. While potatoes roast, heat a large drizzle of olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat.
11. Add green pepper, sliced onion, and a pinch of salt and pepper.
12. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and lightly charred, 5-7 minutes.
13. TIP: Add splashes of water as needed to prevent veggies from burning.
14. Transfer to a plate.
15. Meanwhile, remove pork sausage* from casing if necessary; discard casing.
16. Heat a drizzle of olive oil in pan used for veggies over medium-high heat.
17. Add pork sausage and cook, breaking up meat into pieces, until browned and cooked through, 4-6 minutes.
18. TIP: If there’s excess grease in your pan, carefully pour it out.
19. Stir tomato paste, half the Italian Seasoning, and 1⁄2 cup water into pan with sausage.
20. (For 4 servings, use all the Italian Seasoning and 1 cup water.) Cook, stirring, until sauce is bubbling, 1-2 minutes.
21. Taste and season with salt and pepper.
22. When potatoes have about 5 minutes left, halve baguettes lengthwise, stopping before you get all the way through.
23. Spread cut sides with garlic butter.
24. Place, cut sides up, on a second baking sheet.
25. TIP: Line sheet with foil first for easier cleanup.
26. Toast on middle rack of oven until bread is golden, 3-4 minutes.
27. Carefully spoon sausage mixture onto bottom halves; top with veggies and mozzarella.
28. TIP: The sandwiches are supposed to be messy—don’t be afraid to pile them high!
29. Return to middle rack until cheese melts, 2-3 minutes more.
30. Close heroes, pressing down to secure. Divide between plates. Serve with potato wedges on the side.
