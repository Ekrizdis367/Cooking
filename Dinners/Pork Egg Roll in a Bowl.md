## Ingredients

- 2 Apricot Jam #Condiment
- 2 oz Sweet Thai Chili Sauce #Sauce
- 4 tbsp Soy Sauce #Condiment
- 12 ml Ponzu Sauce #Condiment
- 4 tbsp Sesame Oil #Condiment
- 8 oz Shredded Carrots #Produce
- 8 oz Shredded Red Cabbage #Produce
- 8 oz Bok Choy and Napa Cabbage #Produce
- 20 oz Ground Pork #Meat

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, combine jam, chili sauce, half the soy sauce, and 2 TBSP hot water (4 TBSP for 4 servings). Set apricot-chili duck sauce aside.
2. In a separate small bowl, combine ponzu, half the sesame oil, remaining soy sauce, and 1⁄4 tsp sugar (1⁄2 tsp for 4). Set sesame-soy mixture aside.
3. Heat a drizzle of oil in a large pan over medium-high heat.
4. Add carrots, red cabbage, and bok choy and napa cabbage to hot pan.
5. Cook, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned and tender, 3-5 minutes.
6. Remove from heat.
7. Transfer veggie stir-fry to a medium bowl. Stir in remaining sesame oil and a pinch of salt and pepper. Wipe out pan.
8. Heat a drizzle of oil in same pan over medium-high heat.
9. Add pork*; using a spatula, press into an even layer.
10. Cook, undisturbed, until browned on bottom, 3-4 minutes.
11. Break up meat into pieces and continue cooking until pork is cooked through, 1-2 minutes.
12. Stir in sesame-soy mixture. Cook until flavors meld, 1-2 minutes more.
13. Divide veggie stir-fry between bowls. Top with pork. Drizzle with apricot-chili duck sauce and serve.
