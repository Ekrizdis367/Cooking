## Ingredients

- 1 cup Jasmine Rice #Grain
- 2 Onion #Produce
- 2 Long Green Pepper #Produce
- 20 oz Ground Turkey #Meat
- 2 Tex-Mex Paste #Seasoning
- 2 Tomato #Produce
- 1 Lime #Produce
- 1/4 Cilantro #Produce
- 4 tbsp Smoky Red Pepper Crema #Condiment

## Instructions

1. In a small pot, combine rice, ¾ cup water (1½ cups for 4 servings), and a pinch of salt.
2. Bring to a boil, then cover and reduce to a low simmer.
3. Cook until rice is tender, 15-18 minutes.
4. Keep covered off heat until ready to serve.
5. While rice cooks, wash and dry produce.
6. Finely dice tomato.
7. Halve, peel, and cut onion into ½-inch-thick wedges; mince one wedge until you have 1 TBSP (2 TBSP for 4 servings).
8. Zest and quarter lime.
9. Core, deseed, and dice green pepper into ½-inch pieces.
10. Mince cilantro.
11. In a small bowl, combine tomato, minced onion, and a big squeeze of lime juice; season with salt.
12. Add smoky red pepper crema to a separate small bowl. Stir in water 1 tsp at a time until mixture reaches a drizzling consistency.
13. Heat a drizzle of oil in a medium pan (use a large pan for 4 servings) over medium-high heat.
14. Add green pepper and onion wedges.
15. Season with salt.
16. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and lightly charred, 7-9 minutes.
17. Transfer to a plate.
18. Heat a drizzle of oil in same pan over medium-high heat. Add pork* and a big pinch of salt. Cook, breaking up meat into pieces, until browned, 4-6 minutes.
19. Stir in Tex-Mex paste and ¼ cup water (1/3 cup for 4 servings) until combined.
20. Bring to a simmer and cook until mixture is saucy and pork is cooked through, 1-2 minutes more.
21. Fluff rice with a fork; stir in lime zest and half the cilantro.
22. Season with salt and pepper.
23. TIP: For extra richness, stir in 1 TBSP butter (2 TBSP for 4 servings).
24. Divide rice between bowls and top with veggies, saucy pork, salsa, crema, remaining cilantro, and any remaining sauce from pan.
25. Serve with remaining lime wedges on the side.
