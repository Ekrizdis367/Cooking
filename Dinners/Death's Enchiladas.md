## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp Neutral Cooking Oil #Oil
- 8 Flour Tortillas #Bread
- 1 Medium Onion, Chopped #Produce
- 2 Jalapeños, Seeded and Diced #Produce
- 2 Chicken Breast #Meat
- One 10-Ounce Can Green Enchilada Sauce #Condiment
- 1 cup Shredded Cheese #Dairy
- 1 cup Salsa Verde for Serving #Condiment

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. In a medium sauté pan or skillet over medium-high heat, heat a small amount of the 3 tablespoons of oil.
3. Fry the tortillas, one at a time, until lightly golden brown, about 15 seconds per side.
4. Transfer to paper towels to drain.
5. In the same pan over medium heat, cook and stir the onions and jalapeños until soft, about 5 minutes, adding a touch more oil if necessary.
6. Add the chicken, stir to combine, and cook another 2 minutes.
7. Remove from the heat and stir in queso fresco or other shredded cheese.
8. Divide the filling evenly among the tortillas and roll them into long cylinders.
9. Place each one, seam-side down, into a 9-by-13-inch baking dish.
10. Cover the enchiladas with the enchilada sauce, and top with the cotija.
11. Bake until golden brown, 15 to 20 minutes. Remove from the oven and serve with salsa verde.
