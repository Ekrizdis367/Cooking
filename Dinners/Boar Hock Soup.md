# Ingredients

- [ ] 2 pounds pork hocks, rinsed and patted dry #Meat  
- [ ] Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper #Seasoning  
- [ ] 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil #Oil  
- [ ] 1 medium yellow onion, chopped #Produce  
- [ ] 2 medium carrots, chopped #Produce  
- [ ] 2 celery stalks, chopped #Produce  
- [ ] 2 medium russet potatoes, peeled and diced #Produce  
- [ ] 3 cloves garlic, minced #Produce  
- [ ] 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves #Herb  
- [ ] 2 bay leaves #Herb  
- [ ] 8 cups low-sodium chicken broth #Broth  
- [ ] 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar #Condiment  
- [ ] ¼ cup chopped fresh parsley #Herb  
- [ ] Baguette loaf #Bread 

# Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.  
2. Sprinkle the pork all over with 1½ teaspoons salt and ½ teaspoon pepper.  
3. Arrange the pork in a large roasting pan and roast until the pork is well browned on the bottom, about 30 minutes. Turn the pork and continue roasting until the pork is browned all over, about 30 minutes more. Transfer to a large bowl and set aside.  
4. Reduce the oven temperature to 300°F.  
5. In a large Dutch oven or other heavy pot, heat the olive oil over medium-high heat. Add the onion, carrots, and celery and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 10 minutes. Add the potatoes and garlic and cook for 2 minutes more.  
6. Add the thyme, bay leaves, broth, and vinegar, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Return the pork to the pot. Bring to a simmer over medium heat, then reduce to low, cover, and simmer until the pork is tender, about 1½ hours.  
7. Transfer the pork to a board and let cool slightly. Shred the meat, discarding the skin, fat, and bones. Return the shredded meat to the pot. Simmer for 10 minutes to heat through.  
8. Remove the bay leaves. Season to taste with salt and pepper, and stir in the parsley before serving.
