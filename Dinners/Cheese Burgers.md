## Ingredients

- 1 lb Ground Beef #Meat
- 4 Hamburger Buns #Bread
- 4 slice Cheese #Cheese
- 1 Onion #Produce
- 2 Tomatoes #Produce
- Lettuce #Produce
- 4 tbsp Mayonnaise #Condiment
- 2 tbsp Ketchup #Condiment

## Instructions

1. Form ground beef into 4 patties and season with salt and pepper.
2. Cook patties on a grill or skillet over medium-high heat, 4-5 minutes per side.
3. Add cheese slices in the last minute of cooking to melt.
4. Toast buns lightly.
5. Assemble burgers with lettuce, tomato, onion, and condiments.
