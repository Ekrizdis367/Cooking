# Ingredients

- [ ] 2 lb Boneless Ribeye Steak #Meat
- [ ] 1 Yellow Onion #Produce 
- [ ] 1 Green Bell Pepper #Produce 
- [ ] Provolone Cheese #Dairy 
- [ ] Hoagie Buns #Bread 

# Instructions

1. If using whole steak, trim and slice into 3" sections. Freeze for one hour to firm meat. Trim thin slices against the grain.
2. Roughly chop thin sliced steak into smaller chunks.
3. Heat skillet to medium-high heat.
4. Dice onion.
5. Oil skillet, add steak and onion. Cooking without stirring till one side is well browned. 4-6 minutes. 
6. Continue to cook, stirring frequently. 
7. Season with salt and pepper.
8. Add a little of the cheese and mix into meat mixture.
9. Turn off heat and separate into individual portion sizes. Top with 2 slices of cheese and allow to melt. 
10. Place each portion onto a hoagie roll and serve.
