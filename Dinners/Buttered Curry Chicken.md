## Ingredients

- 3 cup Jasmine Rice #Baking
- ½ oz Cilantro #Produce
- Garlic Powder #Seasoning
- 40 oz Diced Chicken Thighs #Meat
- 4 Unit Pita Bread #Bread
- Cauliflower #Produce
- 1 jar Butter Chicken Sauce #Sauce
- 1 cup Mozzarella Cheese #Dairy
- 8 tbsp Yogurt #Dairy

## Instructions

1. Wash and dry produce.
2. In a medium pot (large pot for 8 servings), combine rice, 2 1⁄4 cups water (41⁄2 cups for 8), and a pinch of salt.
3. Bring to a boil, then cover and reduce to a low simmer.
4. Cook until rice is tender, 15-18 minutes. Keep covered off heat until ready to serve.
5. While rice cooks, place 2 TBSP butter (4 TBSP for 8 servings) in a small microwave-safe bowl; cover with plastic wrap.
6. Microwave until melted, 30 seconds.
7. Add half the garlic powder (you’ll use the rest later) and stir to combine.
8. Set aside.
9. Roughly chop cilantro.
10. Open package of chicken* and drain off any excess liquid.
11. Heat a large drizzle of oil in a large, preferably high-sided, pan over medium-high heat (use a second large pot for 8 servings).
12. Add chicken in a single layer and season with salt and pepper.
13. Cook, stirring occasionally, until browned and cooked through, 6-8 minutes (8-10 minutes for 8).
14. Reduce heat to medium.
15. Add jar of sauce. Cook, stirring occasionally, until sauce has thickened, 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat. Cover to keep warm.
16. Adjust rack to top position and heat broiler to high. Place pitas on a baking sheet. Spoon garlic butter over tops and sprinkle with mozzarella.
17. Broil garlic pitas until cheese melts and pitas are toasted, 3-4 minutes.
18. TIP: The broiler is a powerful tool!
19. Keep an eye on the pitas to prevent them from burning.
20. Quarter cheesy garlic flatbreads.
21. Fluff rice with a fork.
22. Divide rice between bowls and top with butter chicken.
23. Drizzle with yogurt.
24. Garnish with cilantro.
25. Serve with cheesy garlic flatbreads on the side.
26. > [!note]
27. > Chicken is fully cooked when internal temperature reaches 165°.
