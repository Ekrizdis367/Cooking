## Ingredients

- 1 lb Short Pasta (Such as Rotini or Cavatappi) #Grain
- 2 tbsp Unsalted Butter #Dairy
- 3 tbsp All-Purpose Flour #Baking
- 2 tsp Paprika #Seasoning
- ½ tsp Cayenne Pepper #Seasoning
- 2½ Cups Whole Milk #Dairy
- 1 cup Heavy Cream #Dairy
- 2 tsp Dijon Mustard #Condiment
- 1 tsp Salt #Seasoning #IgnoreIngredient
- ½ tsp Black Pepper #Seasoning #IgnoreIngredient
- 2 cup Shredded Sharp Cheddar Cheese #Dairy
- 1 cup Shredded Gruyère Cheese #Dairy
- ½ cup Grated Parmesan Cheese #Dairy
- ¾ cup Panko Breadcrumbs #Bread
- 1 tbsp Olive Oil #Oil #IgnoreIngredient

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Butter a large baking dish or cast-iron pan and set aside.
2. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the pasta and cook until al dente, about 1 minute less than the package directions. Drain and set aside.
3. In a large saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat.
4. Add the flour, paprika, and cayenne, and whisk until fragrant, about 1 minute.
5. Gradually whisk in the milk and cream, stirring constantly until thickened, 4 to 5 minutes.
6. Stir in Dijon mustard, salt, and pepper. Reduce heat to low and gradually add the cheddar, Gruyère, and Parmesan, stirring until melted and smooth.
7. Add the cooked pasta and toss until evenly coated. Pour the mixture into the prepared baking dish.
8. In a small bowl, combine panko and olive oil, then sprinkle evenly over the top.
9. Bake for 20 minutes, until bubbly and golden brown on top. Let cool slightly before serving.

## Notes

- For Extra Flavor, Mix a Pinch of Smoked Paprika into the Panko Topping.
- This Dish Reheats Beautifully and Makes Excellent Leftovers.
