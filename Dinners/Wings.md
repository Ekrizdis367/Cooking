## Ingredients

- 2 lb Chicken Wings #Meat
- 4 tbsp Butter #Dairy
- 3 clove Garlic #Produce
- 1/2 cup Buffalo Sauce #Sauce
- 1 tbsp Baking Powder #Baking
- 1 tbsp Garlic Powder #Seasoning
- 1 tsp Salt #Seasoning #IgnoreIngredient
- 1 tsp Black Pepper #Seasoning #IgnoreIngredient
- 1 tsp Paprika #Seasoning
- Mac N Cheese #Pasta

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
2. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and add wings. Boil for 8 minutes.
3. Remove wings from water and pat completely dry with paper towels.
4. In a large bowl, combine baking powder, garlic powder, salt, pepper, and paprika.
5. Add wings to bowl and toss to coat evenly with seasoning mixture.
6. Place wings on a baking sheet and bake for 25-30 minutes until crispy and golden brown.
7. While wings bake, melt butter and mix with minced garlic and buffalo sauce.
8. Toss cooked wings with buffalo garlic butter sauce before serving.
