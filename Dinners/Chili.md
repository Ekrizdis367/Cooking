## Ingredients

- 2 lb Ground Beef #Meat
- 1 Onion #Produce
- 3 clove Garlic #Produce
- 2 Bell Peppers #Produce
- 2 can Diced Tomatoes #Canned
- 2 can Kidney Beans #Canned
- 1 can Tomato Paste #Canned
- 2 tbsp Chili Powder #Seasoning
- 1 tsp Cumin #Seasoning
- 2 cup Beef Broth #Canned
- Cornbread #Baking

## Instructions

1. Brown ground beef in a large pot, breaking it up as it cooks.
2. Add diced onion, garlic, and bell peppers. Cook until softened.
3. Stir in tomato paste, chili powder, and cumin. Cook 1 minute.
4. Add diced tomatoes, kidney beans, and beef broth.
5. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 1-2 hours.
6. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
