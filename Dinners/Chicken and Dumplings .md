# Ingredients

- [ ] 1 Whole Chicken #Meat
- [ ] 2 Chicken Bouillon Cubes #Seasoning
- [ ] 1.5 cups Potatoes #Produce
- [ ] 2.5 cups Carrots #Produce
- [ ] 2 tbsp Bisquick #Baking
- [ ] Water #Pantry
- [ ] Salt #Seasoning
- [ ] Pepper #Seasoning

# Instructions

1. Place whole chicken in a large pot and cover with water. Bring to a boil and cook until done, about 90 minutes. Once the water is hot, add chicken bouillon cubes.

2. Remove chicken from pot and place on a platter to cool. Turn off heat under the pot while chicken cools.

3. When chicken is cool enough to handle, remove and discard all skin. Tear meat off the bones into bite-sized pieces; set aside.

4. Turn heat back on under the pot. When broth begins to boil, prepare the thickener by mixing Bisquick with enough water to create a thin, milk-like liquid. Whisk thickener into the boiling broth until well combined.

5. Add diced potatoes and carrots to the pot. (Using more carrots adds sweetness; smaller carrots tend to be sweeter.)

6. Immediately prepare Bisquick dumplings according to package directions. Do not overwork the dough or dumplings will be dense instead of fluffy.

7. Once potatoes and carrots are tender, add chicken back into the pot. Keep broth at a light boil.

8. Drop dumplings gently into the boiling broth. They will sink first, then rise to the top as they cook.

9. Use a fork to separate dumplings as needed. Dumplings are done when the center looks like bread rather than dough.

10. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve hot.
