## Ingredients

- One 12-Ounce Can Buttermilk Biscuits #Bread
- 1 Large Yellow Onion, Diced #Produce
- 1 lb Ground Beef #Meat
- 1 lb Ground Pork #Meat
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire Sauce #Condiment
- ½ cup Ketchup #Condiment
- ½ cup Mustard #Condiment
- ¾ cup Dill Pickles, Chopped #Condiment
- 8 slice American Cheese #Dairy
- Corn Chips for Garnishing (Optional) #Snack

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Cut each raw biscuit into 6 or 8 pieces, and scatter across the bottom of a 9-by-13-inch baking dish.
3. In a large sauté pan or skillet over medium heat, cook the onion, beef, and pork until browned, about 8 minutes.
4. Add the Worcestershire sauce, ketchup, and mustard, and cook another 3 to 4 minutes, until the mixture thickens slightly.
5. Remove from the heat and stir in the pickles.
6. Pour the mixture over the biscuits and top with the cheese.
7. Bake for about 35 to 40 minutes, until golden brown.
8. In the last 5 minutes of baking, scatter crushed corn chips across the top, if using, or add them when the casserole comes out of the oven.
