## Ingredients

- 1 lb Fettuccini Pasta
- 1 tbsp Butter
- 1 lb Cooked Shrimp - Peeled and Deveined
- 4 clove Garlic, Minced
- 1 cup Half-And-Half
- 6 tbsp Grated Parmesan Cheese
- 1 tbsp Chopped Fresh Parsley
- Salt to Taste #IgnoreIngredient

## Instructions

1. Fill a large pot with lightly salted water and bring to a rolling boil. Cook fettuccine at a boil until tender yet firm to the bite, about 8 minutes. Drain.
2. Heat butter in a large skillet over medium heat.
3. Cook and stir shrimp and garlic in butter for 1 minute.
4. Pour in half-and-half; stir.
5. Add Parmesan cheese, 1 tablespoon at a time, stirring constantly.
6. Mix in parsley; season with salt.
7. Simmer, stirring frequently, until sauce is thickened.
8. Stir fettuccine into sauce until evenly coated; serve hot.
