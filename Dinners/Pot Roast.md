# Ingredients

- [ ] 3 lb Chuck Roast #Meat
- [ ] 6 Potatoes #Produce
- [ ] 3 Carrots #Produce
- [ ] 1 Onion #Produce
- [ ] 3 cloves Garlic #Produce
- [ ] 2 cups Beef Broth #Canned
- [ ] 2 tbsp Olive Oil #Condiment
- [ ] 2 tsp Thyme #Seasoning
- [ ] Dijon Mustard #Condiment 

# Instructions

1. Season roast with salt and pepper. Heat oil in Dutch oven and sear roast on all sides.
2. Add diced onion and garlic, cook until softened.
3. Add beef broth and thyme. Bring to a boil.
4. Cover and cook in 325°F oven for 2 hours.
5. Add quartered potatoes and carrots.
6. Cook another 1-1.5 hours until meat is tender and vegetables are cooked.
