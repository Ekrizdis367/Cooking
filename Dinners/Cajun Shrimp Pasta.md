## Ingredients

- 12 oz Pasta ((Linguine, Penne, Fettuccine) (338g))
- 1½ Pounds Raw Large Shrimp (Peeled and Deveined (675g))
- 1½ Tablespoons Cajun Seasoning (Divided)
- ½ tsp Salt #IgnoreIngredient
- ½ tsp Ground Black Pepper #IgnoreIngredient
- 2 tbsp Unsalted Butter
- 1 tbsp Olive Oil #IgnoreIngredient
- 4 Garlic Cloves (Minced)
- 1½ Cups Heavy Cream ((360mL))
- ½ cup Canned Fire-Roasted Diced Tomatoes (Drained (121g))
- ½ cup Grated Parmesan Cheese (Plus More to Serve (40g))
- Chopped Fresh Parsley (for Garnish)

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta according to the package directions. Reserve ¼ cup of pasta water, then drain the pasta well.
2. While the pasta is cooking, season the shrimp with 1 tablespoon of cajun seasoning, salt, and pepper.
3. Heat the butter and oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
4. Add the shrimp and cook, tossing occasionally, until they are pink and opaque, 2 to 3 minutes.
5. Using a slotted spoon, remove the shrimp from the skillet and set aside.
6. Reduce to medium heat.
7. Add the garlic to the skillet and stir for 30 seconds.
8. Stir in the cream, tomatoes, ¼ cup reserved pasta water, and remaining ½ tablespoon cajun seasoning.
9. Bring to a simmer and gradually stir in the cheese until melted.
10. Continue to simmer until the sauce thickens, about 2 minutes.
11. Add the shrimp and cooked pasta to the skillet and toss to coat in the sauce. Serve immediately with more cheese and garnished with parsley, if desired.
