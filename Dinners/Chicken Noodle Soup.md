## Ingredients

- 1 Rotisserie Chicken #Meat
- 16 oz Egg Noodles #Pasta
- 2 Carrots #Produce
- 2 Celery Stalks #Produce
- 1 Onion #Produce
- 8 cup Chicken Broth #Canned
- 2 tsp Dried Thyme #Seasoning
- 2 Bay Leaves #Seasoning

## Instructions

1. Dice onion, carrots, and celery.
2. Cook chicken breast in boiling water until done, then shred.
3. In a large pot, sauté vegetables until softened.
4. Add chicken broth, thyme, and bay leaves. Bring to a boil.
5. Add noodles and cook until tender.
6. Add shredded chicken and simmer 5 minutes.
7. Remove bay leaves and season with salt and pepper.
