## Ingredients

- Kosher Salt #Seasoning #IgnoreIngredient
- 1 lb Dried Spaghetti #Grain
- ½ cup (120 Ml) Extra-Virgin Olive Oil #Oil #IgnoreIngredient
- 10 Garlic Cloves, Thinly Sliced #Produce
- ½ tsp Crushed Red Pepper Flakes #Seasoning
- 2 tbsp Finely Chopped Parsley #Herb
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper #Seasoning #IgnoreIngredient
- ½ cup (4 Oz) Whole Milk Ricotta Cheese #Dairy

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of generously salted water to a boil over high heat.
2. Add the spaghetti and cook according to package instructions, 10 to 12 minutes.
3. Drain in a colander.
4. Add the oil to a large skillet and warm over medium heat. Add the garlic and red pepper and cook, stirring often, until the garlic is golden, about 2 minutes.
5. Remove the skillet from the heat and add the cooked spaghetti. Toss to evenly coat it with the garlic-infused oil.
6. Transfer the pasta onto serving plates and top each portion with parsley, ricotta, and a drizzle of olive oil. Finish with additional black pepper.

## Notes

- For a Richer Flavor, You Can Use Roasted Garlic Instead of Raw Sliced Garlic.
- This Pasta Pairs Beautifully with a Light White Wine or Crisp Ale.
