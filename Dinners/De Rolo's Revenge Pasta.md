# Ingredients

- [ ] Kosher salt #Seasoning  
- [ ] 1 pound dried spaghetti #Grain  
- [ ] ½ cup (120 ml) extra-virgin olive oil #Oil  
- [ ] 10 garlic cloves, thinly sliced #Produce  
- [ ] ½ teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes #Seasoning  
- [ ] 2 tablespoons finely chopped parsley #Herb  
- [ ] Freshly ground black pepper #Seasoning  
- [ ] ½ cup (4 oz) whole milk ricotta cheese #Dairy  

# Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of generously salted water to a boil over high heat. Add the spaghetti and cook according to package instructions, 10 to 12 minutes. Drain in a colander.  
2. Add the oil to a large skillet and warm over medium heat. Add the garlic and red pepper and cook, stirring often, until the garlic is golden, about 2 minutes.  
3. Remove the skillet from the heat and add the cooked spaghetti. Toss to evenly coat it with the garlic-infused oil.  
4. Transfer the pasta onto serving plates and top each portion with parsley, ricotta, and a drizzle of olive oil. Finish with additional black pepper.  

# Notes

- For a richer flavor, you can use roasted garlic instead of raw sliced garlic.  
- This pasta pairs beautifully with a light white wine or crisp ale.
