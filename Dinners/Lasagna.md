# Ingredients

- [ ] 12 Lasagna Noodles #Pasta
- [ ] 1 lb Ground Beef #Meat
- [ ] 1 jar Marinara Sauce #Sauce
- [ ] 15 oz Ricotta Cheese #Cheese
- [ ] 2 cups Mozzarella Cheese #Cheese
- [ ] 1/2 cup Parmesan Cheese #Cheese
- [ ] 1 Egg #Dairy
- [ ] 2 tsp Italian Seasoning #Seasoning
- [ ] Garlic Bread Loaf #Bread 

# Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Cook lasagna noodles according to package directions.
2. Brown ground beef in a large skillet, then stir in marinara sauce and Italian seasoning.
3. Mix ricotta cheese with egg in a bowl.
4. Layer in a 9x13 baking dish: sauce, noodles, ricotta mixture, mozzarella. Repeat layers.
5. Top with remaining mozzarella and parmesan cheese.
6. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake 25 more minutes.
7. Let rest 15 minutes before serving.
