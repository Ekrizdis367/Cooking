## Ingredients

- 8 Hot Dogs #Meat
- Flour #Baking
- Yeast #Baking
- 2 tbsp Yellow Mustard #Condiment
- 1 cup Shredded Cheddar Cheese #Cheese

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Split pretzel buns lengthwise and toast until golden.
3. Heat hot dogs according to package directions.
4. Place hot dogs in buns and top with cheese.
5. Bake for 2-3 minutes until cheese melts.
6. Serve with mustard.
