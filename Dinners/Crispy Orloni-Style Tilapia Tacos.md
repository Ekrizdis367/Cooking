## Ingredients

- 2 Lime #Produce
- 1 Jalapeno #Produce
- 6 Radishes #Produce
- Sour Cream #Sauce
- 4 tbsp Mayonnaise #Condiment
- 1 tsp Chipotle Powder #Seasoning
- 8 oz Shredded Red Cabbage #Produce
- 164g Tempura Batter #Baking
- 4 tbsp Blackening Spice #Seasoning
- 22 oz Tilapia #Meat
- 12 Flour Tortillas #Bread

## Instructions

1. Wash and dry produce.
2. Zest and quarter lime (for 4 servings, zest one lime and quarter both).
3. Thinly slice jalapeño into rounds, removing ribs and seeds for less heat.
4. Thinly slice radishes.
5. In a small bowl, combine smoky red pepper crema, mayonnaise, a pinch of lime zest to taste, and chipotle powder to taste.
6. Add water 1 tsp at a time until mixture reaches a drizzling consistency.
7. Season with salt and pepper.
8. In a second small microwave-safe bowl, combine jalapeño, radishes, juice from half the lime, 1 tsp sugar (2 tsp for 4 servings), and a pinch of salt.
9. Microwave for 30 seconds.
10. Set aside, stirring occasionally, until ready to serve.
11. In a medium bowl, combine cabbage, juice from remaining lime, 1 tsp sugar (2 tsp for 4), salt, pepper, and remaining lime zest to taste.
12. In a large bowl, whisk together tempura batter mix, half the Blackening Spice, 1⁄3 cup cold water (2⁄3 cup for 4 servings), and ½ tsp salt (1 tsp for 4) until smooth.
13. TIP: If mixture is too thick, add more water 1 TBSP at a time until it reaches a pancake-batter-like consistency.
14. Pat tilapia* dry with paper towels, then slice into strips (ours were 3 inches long and 1 inch wide).
15. Season all over with remaining Blackening Spice, salt, and pepper.
16. Stir strips into batter until evenly coated.
17. Line a plate with paper towels.
18. Heat a ¼-inch layer of oil in a large, preferably heavy-bottomed, pan over medium-high heat.
19. Once oil is hot enough that a drop of batter sizzles when added to the pan, add coated fish in a single layer, working in batches.
20. (Shake off excess batter before adding.) Cook until golden brown and cooked through, 1-2 minutes per side.
21. TIP: To check for doneness, cut one strip in half.
22. Using tongs or a slotted spoon, transfer to paper-towel-lined plate. Immediately season with salt and pepper.
23. Meanwhile, wrap tortillas in damp paper towels. Microwave until warm and pliable, 30 seconds.
24. Divide tortillas between plates; fill with slaw (draining first) and fried tilapia.
25. Top with chipotle mayo and pickled jalapeño and radishes (draining first) to taste, and serve.
26. > [!warning]
27. > Tilapia is fully cooked when internal temperature reaches 145°.
