## Ingredients

- 8 slice Bread #Bread
- 8 slice Cheddar Cheese #Dairy
- 4 tbsp Butter #Dairy
- 2 can Tomato Soup #Canned
- 1 cup Milk #Dairy

## Instructions

1. Heat tomato soup in a pot with milk according to package directions.
2. Butter one side of each bread slice.
3. Place cheese between bread slices, butter side out.
4. Cook sandwiches in a skillet over medium heat until golden and cheese melts, 3-4 minutes per side.
5. Serve grilled cheese with hot tomato soup.
