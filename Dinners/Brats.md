## Ingredients

- 6 Bratwurst Sausages #Meat
- 6 Hoagie Buns #Bread
- 1 cup Sauerkraut #Canned
- 1 box Mac N Cheese #Pasta
- 2 lb Red Potatoes #Produce
- 4 clove Garlic #Produce
- 6 Strips Bacon #Meat
- 1 tbsp Dijon Mustard #Condiment
- 1 To 3 Tbsp Sugar #Baking
- 1/3 cup Apple Cider Vinegar #Condiment
- 1/2 cup Fresh Parsley #Produce

## Instructions

1. Place bratwurst in a large pot and cover with water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 15-20 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, boil potatoes in salted water for 15-20 minutes until tender. Drain and return to pot, allow to steam dry for a few minutes.
3. Chop bacon and cook in a large pan until crispy. Remove bacon and reserve the grease.
4. Slice potatoes into roughly 1/2 inch chunks.
5. In a small pot, carefully add vinegar, sugar, mustard, salt and pepper to the bacon grease. Simmer and add finely chopped garlic until golden brown.
6. Drizzle sauce over potatoes and toss gently. Add chopped parsley and bacon, toss gently.
7. Remove bratwurst from water and grill or pan-fry until browned on all sides, about 5-7 minutes.
8. Prepare mac and cheese according to package directions.
9. Toast hoagie buns if desired. Serve bratwurst on buns with sauerkraut, alongside warm potato salad and mac and cheese.
