## Ingredients

- 20 oz Ground Pork #Meat
- 2 tsp Dried Thyme #Seasoning
- 2 tsp Garlic Powder #Seasoning
- 8 oz Kale #Produce
- 2 Cannellini Beans #Canned
- 6 Chicken Stock Concentrate #Sauce
- 5 oz Israeli Couscous #Grain
- 4 tbsp Creme Fraiche #Dairy
- 6 tbsp Parmesan Cheese #Dairy

## Instructions

1. Heat a drizzle of oil in a medium pot over medium-high heat.
2. Add pork* and season with thyme, garlic powder, and a pinch of salt and pepper.
3. Cook, breaking up meat into pieces, until browned and cooked through, 4-6 minutes.
4. While pork cooks, wash and dry produce.
5. Drain and rinse beans. Remove and discard any large stems from kale.
6. To pot with pork, add beans, stock concentrates, and 3 cups water (6 cups for 4 servings). Bring to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer.
7. Stir in kale and couscous. Cook, stirring occasionally, until kale is wilted and couscous is tender, 8-10 minutes.
8. Remove from heat and stir in crème fraîche. Taste and season with salt and pepper if desired.
9. Divide soup between bowls. Sprinkle with Parmesan and serve. TIP: If you have a lemon on hand, squeeze some over top for added flavor!
