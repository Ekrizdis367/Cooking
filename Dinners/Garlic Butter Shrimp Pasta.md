## Ingredients

- 8 oz Fettuccine, Linguine, or Spaghetti
- 1 lb Medium Shrimp (Peeled and Deveined)
- Kosher Salt and Freshly Ground Black Pepper (to Taste)
- 8 tbsp Unsalted Butter (Divided)
- 4 clove Garlic (Minced)
- 1/2 tsp Dried Oregano
- 1/2 tsp Crushed Red Pepper Flakes
- 2 cup Baby Arugula or Spinach
- 1/4 cup Freshly Grated Parmesan
- 2 tbsp Chopped Fresh Parsley Leaves

## Instructions

1. In a large pot of boiling salted water, cook pasta according to package instructions; drain well.
2. Season shrimp with salt and pepper, to taste; set aside.
3. Melt 2 tablespoons butter in a large cast iron skillet over medium high heat.
4. Add garlic, oregano and red pepper flakes, and cook, stirring frequently, until fragrant, about 1-2 minutes.
5. Working in batches, add shrimp and cook, stirring occasionally, until pink, about 2-3 minutes; set aside.
6. Melt remaining 6 tablespoons butter in the skillet. Stir in pasta, arugula and Parmesan until arugula begins to wilt, about 2 minutes. Stir in shrimp.
7. Serve immediately, garnished with parsley, if desired.
