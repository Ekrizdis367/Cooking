## Ingredients

- 1 lb . Ground Beef
- ½ cup Finely Chopped Onion
- 1 (1 Ounce) Packet Taco Seasoning Mix
- 1 Large Bag Tortilla Chips ((About 11 Ounces))
- 4 cup Grated Cheese ((Try Cheddar, Monterey Jack, Pepper Jack, Colby, or a Combination of These))
- ½ cup Pickled Jalapeños, (Patted Dry (Optional))
- Optional Toppings: Chopped Tomato, Salsa, Sour Cream, Sliced Black Olives, Chopped Green Onions, Fresh Cilantro, Diced Avocado or Guacamole

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a large rimmed baking sheet with foil or parchment paper and set aside.
2. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, cook the ground beef and onion until the onion is tender and the meat is no longer pink.
3. Drain off the fat and return the beef to the skillet.
4. Add taco seasoning (and water if called for on the packet). Cook according to the package instructions, letting most of the liquid evaporate.
5. Arrange half of the tortilla chips on the prepared baking sheet.
6. Top with half of the cheese, half of the beef mixture, and half of the jalapeños (if using).
7. Repeat layers with remaining ingredients.
8. Bake until the cheese melts and edges just start to turn golden, about 10 to 15 minutes. Watch closely to prevent burning.
9. Remove from oven, add desired toppings, and serve immediately.
