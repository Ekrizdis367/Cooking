# Ingredients

- [ ] 1 lb Chicken Breast #Meat
- [ ] 2 Bell Peppers #Produce
- [ ] 1 Onion #Produce
- [ ] 2 Limes #Produce
- [ ] 8 Flour Tortillas #Bread
- [ ] 2 tbsp Fajita Seasoning #Seasoning
- [ ] 1 cup Shredded Cheese #Cheese
- [ ] 4 tbsp Sour Cream #Dairy
- [ ] 1/4 cup Cilantro #Produce

# Instructions

1. Season chicken with fajita seasoning and cook until done. Slice into strips.
2. Slice bell peppers and onion into strips.
3. Cook vegetables in the same pan until tender-crisp.
4. Warm tortillas in microwave or dry skillet.
5. Serve chicken and vegetables with tortillas, cheese, sour cream, cilantro, and lime wedges.
