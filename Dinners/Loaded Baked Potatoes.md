## Ingredients

- 5 Large Baking Potatoes #Produce
- 8 Strips Bacon #Meat
- 1 cup Sour Cream #Dairy
- 2 cup Shredded Cheese #Cheese
- 2 cup Broccoli Florets #Produce
- 1 cup Shredded Carrots #Produce
- 1 Yellow Onion #Produce
- 4 Green Onions #Produce
- 4 tbsp Butter #Dairy

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Pierce potatoes with a fork and bake for 45-60 minutes until tender.
2. Cook bacon until crispy, then crumble. Reserve some bacon fat.
3. Sauté diced broccoli and carrots in bacon fat until softened.
4. Add chicken broth and milk to cover. Boil for 10 min or until soft. Blend with emulsion blender.
5. Add cheese and allow to melt.
6. Cut open baked potatoes and fluff with a fork.
7. Top with butter, cheese, bacon, broccoli soup, sour cream, and green onions.
