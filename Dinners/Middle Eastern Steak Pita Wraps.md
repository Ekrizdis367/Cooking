## Ingredients

- 1 Red Onion #Produce
- 2 Baby Lettuce #Produce
- 1 Lemon #Produce
- 20 oz Ranch Steak #Meat
- 2 tbsp Shawarma Spice Blend #Seasoning
- 8 oz Grape Tomatoes #Produce
- 1 cup Feta Cheese #Dairy
- 3 oz Greek Vinaigrette #Condiment
- 4 Pita Bread #Bread
- 2 oz Smoky Garlic Herb Sauce #Sauce
- 2 tsp Hot Sauce #Condiment

## Instructions

1. Wash and dry produce.
2. Halve, peel, and thinly slice half the onion (whole onion for 4). Halve lemon. Trim and discard root end from lettuce; chop leaves into bite-size pieces.
3. Pat steak* dry. Thinly slice steak; season with Shawarma Spice Blend, salt, and pepper.
4. Drizzle oil in a hot pan. Add steak; cook, stirring occasionally, until steak reaches desired doneness, 4-6 minutes.
5. While steak cooks, in a microwave- safe bowl, mix onion, juice from half the lemon (whole lemon for 4), and a pinch of salt.
6. Cover with plastic wrap and microwave until onion is tender, 30-40 seconds.
7. Set aside to pickle.
8. In a separate bowl, mix lettuce, tomatoes, feta, and vinaigrette.
9. Wrap pitas in damp paper towels; microwave for 20-30 seconds.
10. Add steak in a line across the middle of each pita.
11. Top with as much salad as you like.
12. Drizzle with smoky garlic herb sauce; top with as much pickled onion (draining first) as you like and as much hot sauce as you like.
13. Fold pitas in half to form wraps. Serve with any remaining salad on the side.
