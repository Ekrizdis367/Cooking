# Ingredients

- [ ] 4 Salmon Fillets #Meat
- [ ] 2 tbsp Olive Oil #Condiment
- [ ] 2 Lemons #Produce
- [ ] 3 cloves Garlic #Produce
- [ ] 2 tbsp Fresh Dill #Produce
- [ ] 1 tsp Paprika #Seasoning

# Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Place salmon fillets on a baking sheet lined with foil.
3. Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice.
4. Season with minced garlic, dill, paprika, salt, and pepper.
5. Bake for 12-15 minutes until fish flakes easily.
6. Serve with lemon wedges.
