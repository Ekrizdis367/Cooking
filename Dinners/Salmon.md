## Ingredients

- 4 Salmon Fillets #Meat
- 2 tbsp Olive Oil #Condiment #IgnoreIngredient
- 2 Lemons #Produce
- 3 clove Garlic #Produce
- 2 tbsp Fresh Dill #Produce
- 1 tsp Paprika #Seasoning
- Asparagus #Produce 
- Brown rice #Grain 
- Soy sauce #Sauce 
- Brown sugar #Baking 
- Garlic #Produce 
- Ginger #Produce 

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Boil 2 cups of rice with 8 cups of water for 30 min. 
3. Place salmon fillets on a baking sheet lined with foil.
4. Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice.
5. Season with minced garlic, dill, paprika, salt, and pepper.
6. Bake for 12-15 minutes until fish flakes easily.
7. Pan fry or bake asparagus. 
8. Simmer 2 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tsp garlic, and 1 tsp grated ginger in a pan for 2 minutes until it forms a sticky glaze. Pour it over the salmon and rice.
9. Serve with lemon wedges.
