## Ingredients

- 1 Onion #Produce
- 1/4 oz Cilantro #Produce
- 1 Lemon #Produce
- 20 oz Ground Pork #Meat
- 2 tbsp Southwest Spice Blend #Seasoning
- 8 oz Shredded Red Cabbage #Produce
- 4 tbsp Mayonnaise #Condiment
- 2 Tex-Mex Pastes #Seasoning
- 12 Flour Tortillas #Bread
- 1/2 cup Monterey Jack Cheese #Dairy
- 4 tbsp Sour Cream #Dairy

## Instructions

1. Wash and dry produce.
2. Halve, peel, and finely dice onion. Finely chop cilantro. Quarter lemon.
3. Heat a drizzle of oil in a large pan over medium-high heat.
4. Add onion and a pinch of salt; cook, stirring occasionally, until onion is slightly softened and lightly browned, 2-3 minutes.
5. Add pork* and Southwest Spice.
6. Cook, breaking up meat into pieces, until browned and cooked through, 4-6 minutes.
7. TIP: If there’s excess grease in your pan, carefully pour it out.
8. While pork cooks, in a large bowl, combine cabbage, mayonnaise, half the cilantro, juice from half the lemon (whole lemon for 4 servings), 1 tsp sugar (2 tsp for 4), and a pinch of salt and pepper.
9. Once pork is cooked through, stir Tex-Mex paste into pan until fully coated. Taste and season with salt and pepper. Remove pan from heat.
10. Wrap tortillas in damp paper towels and microwave until warm and pliable, 30 seconds.
11. Divide tortillas between plates and fill with pork filling, Monterey Jack, slaw, sour cream, and remaining cilantro.
12. Serve tacos with any remaining lemon wedges on the side.
