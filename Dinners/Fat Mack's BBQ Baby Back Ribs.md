# Ingredients

## Rub
- [ ] ¼ cup granulated sugar #Baking  
- [ ] ¼ cup loosely packed light brown sugar #Baking  
- [ ] 2 tablespoons salt #Seasoning  
- [ ] 2 tablespoons black pepper #Seasoning  
- [ ] 1 teaspoon ground cumin #Seasoning  
- [ ] 1 teaspoon dry mustard #Seasoning  
- [ ] 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper #Seasoning  
- [ ] 1 teaspoon chili powder #Seasoning  
- [ ] 1 teaspoon garlic powder #Seasoning  

## Ribs
- [ ] 2 racks pork baby back ribs #Meat  
- [ ] 2 cups smoky barbecue sauce #Condiment  

# Instructions

1. To make the dry rub: Combine all the ingredients in a medium bowl and mix well. Set aside.  
2. Preheat the oven to 250°F.  
3. To make the ribs: Wash and dry the ribs, and remove the membrane from the back with a sharp knife. Place the ribs individually on sheets of aluminum foil, then coat all sides with the dry rub. Wrap the ribs tightly in the foil.  
4. Bake the ribs until cooked through, about 2 hours; then remove from the oven and let them sit 15 minutes to rest. Increase the oven temperature to 350°F.  
5. Open the foil, drain the juices, and brush the top of the ribs with barbecue sauce. Return to the oven, with the foil open, and cook 40 more minutes, brushing often with sauce.  
6. Remove from the oven and allow to rest for 10 minutes. Transfer to a cutting board, cut into individual ribs, and serve.
