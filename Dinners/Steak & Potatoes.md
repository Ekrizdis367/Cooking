# Ingredients

- [ ] 4 Ribeye Steaks #Meat 
- [ ] 2 lbs Baby Potatoes #Produce 
- [ ] 1 lb Broccoli #Produce
- [ ] 3 tbsp Olive Oil #Condiment
- [ ] 2 tbsp Butter #Dairy
- [ ] 2 tsp Garlic Powder #Seasoning 

# Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Cut potatoes in half and toss with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
2. Roast potatoes for 25-30 minutes until golden.
3. Season steaks with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
4. Heat a skillet over high heat and sear steaks 3-4 minutes per side for medium-rare.
5. Steam broccoli until tender, about 5 minutes.
6. Let steaks rest 5 minutes, then serve with potatoes and broccoli.
