## Ingredients

- 2 Zucchini #Produce
- 2 tbsp Italian Seasoning #Seasoning
- 20 oz Ground Turkey #Meat
- 12 oz Spaghetti #Pasta
- 2 Units Crushed Tomatoes #Sauce
- 2 Chicken Stock Concentrate #Sauce
- 3 tbsp Sour Cream #Dairy
- 6 tbsp Parmesan Cheese #Dairy

## Instructions

1. Heat broiler to high. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Wash and dry produce.
2. Trim and halve zucchini lengthwise; thinly slice crosswise into half-moons.
3. Toss zucchini on a baking sheet with a large drizzle of olive oil, half the Italian Seasoning (you’ll use the rest in the next step), salt, and pepper.
4. Broil until browned and softened, 5-7 minutes. TIP: Watch carefully to avoid burning.
5. Meanwhile, heat a drizzle of olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat.
6. Add sausage* and remaining Italian Seasoning.
7. Cook, breaking up meat into pieces, until browned and cooked through, 4-6 minutes.
8. Once water is boiling, add spaghetti to pot. Cook, stirring occasionally, until al dente, 9-11 minutes.
9. Reserve 1⁄2 cup pasta cooking water, then drain and set aside.
10. While pasta cooks, stir crushed tomatoes, stock concentrate, and 1 tsp sugar (2 tsp for 4 servings) into pan with sausage.
11. Season generously with salt (we used 1⁄2 tsp; 3⁄4 tsp for 4) and pepper.
12. Bring to a simmer and cook until reduced, 5-7 minutes.
13. Stir sour cream and 1⁄2 TBSP butter (1 TBSP for 4 servings) into pan with sauce until melted and combined.
14. Add zucchini and drained spaghetti; toss to coat.
15. (TIP: If your pan isn’t large enough, carefully pour everything back into empty pasta pot.) If needed, stir in reserved pasta cooking water 1 TBSP at a time until pasta is thoroughly coated in sauce.
16. Taste and season with salt and pepper.
17. Divide pasta between bowls. Sprinkle with Parmesan and serve.
