# Ingredients

- [ ] 1 lb Chicken Breast #Meat 
- [ ] 8 Flour Tortillas #Bread
- [ ] 2 cups Shredded Cheese #Cheese 
- [ ] 4 tbsp Sour Cream #Dairy 
- [ ] Chipotle #Sauce 
- [ ] 5 Ears Corn #Produce 
- [ ] 1/2 cup Cotija Cheese #Cheese 
- [ ] 2 Limes #Produce 

# Instructions

1. Season and cook chicken breast until done, then dice.
2. Grill corn and cut kernels off the cob. Mix with cotija cheese and lime juice.
3. Place shredded cheese and diced chicken on half of each tortilla.
4. Fold tortillas in half and cook in a skillet until golden and cheese melts, 3-4 minutes per side.
5. Serve with corn salad and sour cream.
