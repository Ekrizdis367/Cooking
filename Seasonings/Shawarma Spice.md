## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp Smoked Paprika
- 1 tbsp Coriander
- 2 tbsp Cinnamon
- 1 1/3 tsp Cumin
- 2 tsp Powdered Ginger
- 1 tsp Black Pepper #IgnoreIngredient
- 1/2 tsp Clove
- 1/2 tsp Turmeric
- 1/2 tsp Salt #IgnoreIngredient

## Instructions

1. Add all ingredients to a small jar or bowl. Mix well with a fork.
