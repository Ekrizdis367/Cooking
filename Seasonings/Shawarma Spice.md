# Ingredients

- [ ] 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- [ ] 1 tbsp coriander
- [ ] 2 tbsp cinnamon
- [ ] 1 1/3 tsp cumin
- [ ] 2 tsp powdered ginger
- [ ] 1 tsp black pepper
- [ ] 1/2 tsp clove
- [ ] 1/2 tsp turmeric
- [ ] 1/2 tsp salt

# Instructions

Add all ingredients to a small jar or bowl. Mix well with a fork.
