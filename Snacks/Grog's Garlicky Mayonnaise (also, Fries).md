# Ingredients

## Oven Fries
- [ ] 2 pounds russet potatoes, peeled #Produce  
- [ ] 3 tablespoons neutral oil (such as canola) #Oil  
- [ ] 1 teaspoon kosher salt #Seasoning  
- [ ] Freshly ground black pepper, to taste #Seasoning  
- [ ] 2 tablespoons finely grated Parmesan cheese #Dairy  
- [ ] 2 tablespoons chopped parsley (for garnish, optional) #Herb  

## Garlicky Mayonnaise
- [ ] 1 large egg yolk #Egg  
- [ ] 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard #Condiment  
- [ ] 1 cup neutral oil (such as canola or grapeseed) #Oil  
- [ ] 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice #Produce  
- [ ] 2 garlic cloves, finely grated #Produce  
- [ ] ½ teaspoon kosher salt #Seasoning  
- [ ] Freshly ground black pepper, to taste #Seasoning  

# Instructions

## Oven Fries
1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.  
2. Cut the potatoes lengthwise into ½-inch-thick sticks and place in a large bowl. Cover with cold water and soak for at least 30 minutes to remove excess starch. Drain and pat dry with paper towels.  
3. Toss the potatoes with the oil, salt, and pepper until evenly coated. Arrange in a single layer on two parchment-lined baking sheets.  
4. Bake for 20 minutes, flip the fries, and bake another 20 to 25 minutes, until golden and crisp.  
5. Remove from the oven, sprinkle with Parmesan, and garnish with parsley, if using. Serve warm.  

## Garlicky Mayonnaise
1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the egg yolk and mustard.  
2. Very slowly drizzle in the oil while whisking constantly, starting a few drops at a time, until the mixture begins to emulsify and thicken.  
3. Once about half the oil is incorporated, whisk in the lemon juice. Continue slowly adding the remaining oil until fully combined and smooth.  
4. Stir in the garlic, salt, and pepper to taste.  
5. Serve with the oven fries or store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.  

# Notes

- If you don’t want to make your own mayonnaise, you can stir minced garlic and lemon juice into store-bought mayo for a quick version.  
- These fries are best enjoyed fresh but can be reheated in a hot oven to crisp them up again.
