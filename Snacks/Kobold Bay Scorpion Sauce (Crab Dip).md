## Ingredients

- 1 (8-Ounce) Package Reduced-Fat Cream Cheese #Dairy
- ½ cup Mayonnaise #Condiment
- 2 tsp Old Bay Seasoning #Seasoning
- 1 tbsp Chopped Fresh Dill #Herb
- 1 lb Lump Crabmeat #Seafood
- ½ tsp Hot Sauce #Condiment
- 1 cup Shredded Cheddar Cheese (Plus Extra for Topping) #Dairy

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. In a medium bowl, mix together the cream cheese, mayonnaise, Old Bay seasoning, dill, crabmeat, hot sauce, and ½ cup of the cheddar cheese until well combined.
3. Spread the mixture into an 8-inch baking dish and sprinkle the remaining cheese evenly on top.
4. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, until hot and bubbly.
5. Remove from the oven and serve warm with bread, crackers, or crudités.

## Notes

- For Extra Spice, Add a Dash More Hot Sauce or a Sprinkle of Cayenne.
- This Dip Pairs Beautifully with Toasted Baguette Slices or Pita Chips.
